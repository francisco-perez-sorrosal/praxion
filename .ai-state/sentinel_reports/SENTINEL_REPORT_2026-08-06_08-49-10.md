### Methodology

**This run was a six-agent fan-out: five independent collector lenses plus this synthesis pass.**

*Why.* The prior solo run (2026-08-05) terminated at its `maxTurns: 100` cap having written only a `[pending]` skeleton — an audit completed in context but lost on disk with no error signal. That failure is finding **C1** of this report; the fan-out is the workaround for it.

Collectors were forbidden from reading each other's fragments, which is what made §4's convergence findings meaningful — three lenses agreeing is evidence only if they could not have copied each other.
